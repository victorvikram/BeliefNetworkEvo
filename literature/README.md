# Literature

Shared papers for the two-paper package. Reference manuals such as the GSS codebook stay
in `docs/`; this folder is for work we cite and argue with.

## Present

- `DellaPosta-PluralisticCollapse-2020.pdf` — DellaPosta, Daniel. 2020. "Pluralistic
  Collapse: The 'Oil Spill' Model of Mass **Opinion** Polarization." *American Sociological
  Review* 85(3):507–536. doi:10.1177/0003122420922989. He shows beliefs cluster and that
  clustering increases, using zero-order pairwise correlations. Note he is **not** the
  closest prior work — the 2026-08-14 literature search found that is Baldassarri & Gelman
  (2008), who make the consequence claim, and Cely (2025), who makes it with our estimator.
  DellaPosta treats the structure as the *explanandum* throughout.
  *(Title corrected 2026-08-14: previously recorded as "Mass Political Polarization".)*

### Priority — the two the paper must argue against

- **Baldassarri, Delia, and Andrew Gelman. 2008.** "Partisans without Constraint: Political
  Polarization and Trends in American Public Opinion." *American Journal of Sociology*
  114(2):408–446. doi:10.1086/590649. *The direct predecessor* — makes the consequence claim
  for beliefs, deriving it from Blau. Paywalled.
- **Cely, Tadeas. 2025.** "One more constrained than the other: Asymmetrical ideological
  alignment and its implications for polarization." *European Journal of Political Research*
  64(4):1945–1973. doi:10.1111/1475-6765.70016. *The closest rival* — EBIC-glasso belief networks on partisan
  subpopulations. CC-BY, should be freely available.
